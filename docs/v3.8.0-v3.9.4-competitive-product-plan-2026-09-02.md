## Delivery boundary

This train implements 15 repository-owned product contracts. It does not turn
fixtures, static policies, preview adapters, or local read models into proof of
a paid provider account, a deployed managed service, organization acceptance,
or production authorization. `/experiments` presents these four truth layers
independently.

## Safety corrections

- A MiniMax account alias is no longer treated as verified native image/video
  support from its name alone. The public source currently pins M2.7 text
  routes; account model discovery and a model-specific modality probe are
  required before non-text Benchmark work is admitted.
- Workload routing cannot pick a winner from provider health alone. Missing or
  stale quality evidence makes a candidate ineligible.
- SWIFT and LLaMA-Factory plans validate supported parameters and emit argv
  arrays, but their preview executors remain fail-closed in this checkout.
- Cache prompt tokens are observations, not claimed financial savings, until a
  provider-native cached-token billing receipt is available.
