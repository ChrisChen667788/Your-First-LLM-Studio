signature/fingerprint checks validate metadata shape and digest binding only.
They do not perform public-key verification of a detached signature. The
cryptographic verifier, pinned trust configuration and operator intake are

## Decision

All fifteen repository-owned external-evidence activation contracts are
source-complete. The train defines a relay/handoff-bound activation manifest,
strict signed stage intake, bounded digest-only quarantine, fourteen-domain
currentness, discrepancy and expiry reconciliation, separately pinned
distribution-decision evidence, and a non-authorizing production admission
firewall.

This is a source-completion decision, not a workload-identity, authority,
distribution, or production decision. The current projection is source `15/15`
pass, deterministic local `9/15` pass, persisted manifests `0`, observations
`0`, quarantines `0`, reconciliations `0`, decision requests `0`, decision
receipts `0`, covered milestones `0/14`, external `HOLD`, and production
`BLOCKED`.

## Implemented Scope

- `features/evidence/external-activation.ts` owns strict activation manifests,
  observation and quarantine contracts, freshness/replay defenses,
  reconciliation, decision-request and decision-receipt binding, and default
  fail-closed external intake;
- `features/evidence/external-activation-store.ts` persists bounded,
  idempotent, digest-only activation state without raw credentials, archive
  objects, observation payloads, or signatures;
- feature-owned Experiments read model, API, panel, deterministic export,
  release-train milestones, CI-route coverage, cross-surface smoke coverage,
  and documentation expose source readiness without an unconfigured external
  action.

## Remaining External Evidence

The following are intentionally not represented as completed by source code:

1. a controlled workload identity and issuer-issued identity attestation;
2. a live controlled relay authority, current discovery record, and trust
   continuity/rotation record;
3. immutable archive retention and legal-hold read-back from the storage
   authority;
4. fourteen current, independently issued observer and receipt-verification
   pairs;
5. externally controlled discrepancy resolution and separately authorized
   distribution decision; and
6. a separately controlled production transition and independent execution
   witness.

An in-memory observation, locally persisted activation record, accepted-shaped
decision receipt, or deterministic reconciliation projection cannot replace
those external records or authorize distribution or production.
