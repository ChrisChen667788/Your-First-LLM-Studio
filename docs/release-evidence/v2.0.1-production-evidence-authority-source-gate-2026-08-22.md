## Non-promotion invariant

The verifier only validates a detached signature, an out-of-band pinned trust anchor,
and evidence-bundle structure. Even a complete and verified fixture produces
`authorizationStatus: "not-authorized"` and `productionStatus: "blocked"`. It has no
write endpoint, no private key, and no production transition implementation.

## Remaining external action

The independently operated release authority must supply live receipt artifacts,
perform semantic review of their contents, and decide whether to authorize a real
production transition. This repository reports the verification boundary only.
