## Scope

This record covers all fifteen continuation boundaries from `v5.8.0` through
`v5.9.4`: policy registry, trust snapshots, signed rotation, durable admission,
replay protection, scoped freshness, discrepancy projection, quarantine leases
and fencing, currentness/quorum, decision admission and one-shot consumption,
the distribution/production firewall, portable export, and the reconciled
operator surface.

  operator-authorized mutation and export/restore commands.
- `features/experiments/ActivationOperationsPanel.tsx` exposes configuration,
  revision, trust, quarantine, conflict, decision, and HOLD/BLOCKED state in

## Follow-Up Authority

Before any real activation decision is treated as operationally current,
operators must provision the organization-owned trust and policy files, perform
the independent rotation and quarantine ceremonies, import current fourteen-
domain evidence, and retain the resulting export outside the repository. The
service will continue to hold distribution and block production until those
external authorities are separately evidenced.
