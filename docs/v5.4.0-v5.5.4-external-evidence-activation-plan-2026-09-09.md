# v5.4.0-v5.5.4 External Evidence Activation Plan

Date: 2026-09-09
Status: source-complete, independent external evidence needed
Surface: `/experiments` and `/api/experiments/evidence-external-activation-train`

## Purpose

This fifteen-version train follows the `v5.2.0-v5.3.4` relay boundary. It does
not activate a release or production environment. Its purpose is narrower and
reviewable: define the exact digest-only external evidence that may be admitted
to a later independent decision request.

- an activation manifest pins one relay configuration, handoff, authority,
  trust fingerprint, policy digest, required stages, and bounded expiry;
- observations are strict, signed, digest-only records with an exact manifest,
  stage, optional domain milestone, and validity window;
- all malformed, stale, duplicate, policy-denied, signature-invalid, or
  mismatched material is visible only through a bounded quarantine ledger;
- fourteen-domain currentness requires both observer-attestation and
  receipt-verification records for every source milestone, all global stages,
  no stale observation, and no quarantine;
- a decision request is short-lived and pins a distinct decision authority;
- a decision receipt is still evidence intake, not local distribution or
  production authorization.

## Safety Invariants

1. The default activation intake port always throws `external-unavailable`.
2. No raw credential, archive object, observation payload, or signature is
   persisted by this train; records use SHA-256 digests and bounded metadata.
3. Activation manifests must bind an already pinned relay configuration and
   handoff when evaluated against real stores.
4. Every observation must bind the active manifest, stage shape, signer
   fingerprint, and a maximum 31-day validity window.
5. Observer-attestation and receipt-verification observations require exactly
   one known source milestone; global stages cannot name a milestone.
6. Quarantine blocks a reconciliation from becoming eligible.
7. A decision request must use a separate authority identity and key from the
   activation authority.
8. Even an accepted-looking decision receipt cannot authorize distribution or
   production in this application.

## Evidence and Promotion State

The source gate proves contract ownership and deterministic validation only. It
does not prove a real workload identity, external authority, immutable archive,
independent observer, distribution decision, or production transition. The
default durable stores begin empty; external status remains `HOLD` and
production remains `BLOCKED` until independently issued records are configured,
imported, and verified.

The companion source-gate record is created after the complete test, typecheck,
build, and route-smoke validation pass.
