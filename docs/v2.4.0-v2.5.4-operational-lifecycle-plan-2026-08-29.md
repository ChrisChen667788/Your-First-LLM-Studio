# v2.4.0-v2.5.4 Operational Lifecycle Plan

Date: 2026-08-29

## Scope

This independently bounded 15-version train begins after the v2.3.4 assurance
archive. It adds one capability that the earlier assurance chains deliberately
did not provide: a read-only aggregation of real source-owned runtime and
product signals. Those local signals are shown beside, but never substituted
for, independently signed external evidence.

The truth model remains explicit:

- `sourceStatus: pass` means the verifier, feature adapters, API, UI, export,
  tests, and documentation exist.
- `localStatus: pass | attention` describes current module-owned local signals.
- `externalStatus: hold` means the required organization, customer, operator,
  managed-service, or independent-review records are not all verified.
- `productionStatus: blocked` is invariant. These routes are read-only and have
  no deployment or authorization action.

## Source architecture

- `features/experiments/operational-source-signals.ts` owns the normalized,
  exception-safe signal contract. It imports only read functions from the
  owning Runtime, Provider, Telemetry, Benchmark, Retrieval, Agent, Workflow,
  Fine-tune, and Deployment modules.
- A source read failure becomes `unavailable`; missing observations become
  `attention`. Neither condition throws the whole Experiments surface.
- `features/experiments/ai-operations-intelligence-train.ts` binds nine local
  signals and one external-only review to `v2.4.0-v2.4.9`.
- `features/experiments/deployment-lifecycle-assurance-train.ts` binds four
  local signals and one external-only review to `v2.5.0-v2.5.4`.
- Both trains reuse `external-assurance-chain.ts` for strict schema validation,
  detached RSA-SHA256 signatures, pinned public-key digests, freshness,
  predecessor lineage, semantic checks, durable identity, and independent
  final review.

```text

FIRST_LLM_AI_OPERATIONS_<KEY>_SIGNATURE_PATH
FIRST_LLM_AI_OPERATIONS_<KEY>_PUBLIC_KEY_PATH
FIRST_LLM_AI_OPERATIONS_<KEY>_KEY_SHA256

FIRST_LLM_DEPLOYMENT_LIFECYCLE_<KEY>_PATH
FIRST_LLM_DEPLOYMENT_LIFECYCLE_<KEY>_SIGNATURE_PATH
FIRST_LLM_DEPLOYMENT_LIFECYCLE_<KEY>_PUBLIC_KEY_PATH
FIRST_LLM_DEPLOYMENT_LIFECYCLE_<KEY>_KEY_SHA256
```

The first v2.4 record must bind a verified `v2.3.4` digest. The first v2.5
record must bind a verified `v2.4.9` digest. Final records must be issued by an
organization distinct from every reviewed control issuer.

all local signal summaries and signed-evidence states but does not mint, sign,
approve, or transition any external record.

## External completion boundary

Source completion is possible in this repository. External and production
completion is not. Real managed traffic, telemetry, billing, blind evaluation,
distributed workers, customer KMS/HSM, data-residency attestations, independent
destination import, continuity/exit execution, and organization/customer
sign-off remain `HOLD`; production remains `BLOCKED`.
